Privacy policy

gws CLI · effective 9 November 2026

Who uses this app

gws CLI is a private OAuth client used by a single person, its owner, to access their own Google account with the open-source Google Workspace CLI. It is not offered to anyone else.

What data is accessed

With the owner's consent, the app can read and change the owner's Google Drive files, Docs, Sheets and Slides, and read, draft, send and organize the owner's Gmail messages. It also reads the account's name and email address to identify the signed-in account.

Where the data goes

Requests go directly from the owner's computer to Google's APIs. OAuth tokens are stored on that computer, encrypted. The app has no server of its own, and data obtained from Google APIs is not sold, shared with or transferred to anyone.

The use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Terms of use

The app is provided as is, for the owner's personal use only. No other person is authorized to use it.
