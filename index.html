gws CLI

gws CLI is a private, single-user OAuth client. It lets its owner use the open-source Google Workspace CLI (gws) on their own computer to work with their own Google Drive, Docs, Sheets, Slides and Gmail.

It is not a public service: there is nothing to sign up for, and it is not offered to other users.
